The real enemy isn’t Spotify. It’s standing in front of the shelf thinking, what the hell do I play tonight? Too many records. The same thirty keep winning. The rest sit. Crate puts one on and you stay in the chair.

I built it because I love vinyl and I kept realizing I was listening to the same records while hundreds sat there. Made by a listener. Not a corporation.

What Crate is

  • It remembers the crate — every LP you actually own.
  • It picks one for tonight, with a reason.
  • It keeps the night — a spin log, liner notes, a journal.

What it is not

  • Not a streamer. Not a shop. Not Discogs with a new coat of paint.
  • Not social. Recommendations are anonymous.

No app to download. It is a site, on purpose.
